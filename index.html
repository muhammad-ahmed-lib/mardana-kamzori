How do I contact Hakim Faizan Lohapar?

WhatsApp is the primary contact method. Use any “Chat on WhatsApp” button on this page, or message +92 309 1370220 directly.

Can I ask about ingredients before ordering?

Yes. Please ask for ingredient lists, preparation notes, and recommended usage before you decide. You are encouraged to review that information with a qualified healthcare professional.

Are results the same for everyone?

No. Individual results may vary. Response can depend on overall health, lifestyle, consistency, and other factors. No wellness product works the same way for every person, and nothing on this website is a guaranteed outcome.

Can I use these products if I take other medicines?

If you take prescription or other medicines, have an existing medical condition, or are under clinical care, consult a qualified healthcare professional before using any wellness product. Share the ingredient information with your practitioner.

How is my information handled?

Questions sent on WhatsApp are handled respectfully and privately. Share only what you are comfortable sharing. This website does not collect medical records.
